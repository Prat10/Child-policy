Child Safety & CSAE Policy

Our commitment to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation across CircleApp, and how to report a concern.

Last updated September 28, 2026
Zero-tolerance policy

CircleApp strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation, child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexualization of minors, grooming, and any other form of sexual exploitation or abuse involving children.

CircleApp is committed to maintaining a safe environment for everyone who uses it, especially children and minors. This policy explains what we prohibit, how we respond to reports, and how to reach us if you see something concerning.

01 Prohibited content and behavior

CircleApp does not allow anyone to create, upload, share, request, promote, or distribute:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Sexually explicit content involving minors
  • Sexual exploitation or abuse of children
  • Sexualization of children or minors
  • Grooming, or attempts to build an inappropriate relationship with a minor
  • Requests for sexual content involving minors
  • Content that facilitates trafficking or sexual exploitation of children
  • Attempts to obtain, distribute, or solicit CSAM

02 Reporting a concern

If you suspect child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, or any other child-safety violation on CircleApp, report it to us right away.

When you report an issue, include as much relevant detail as you can — a username, profile link, message, or content — so we can identify the violation quickly.

  1. Open your Profile

    Go to the Profile tab in CircleApp.

  2. Tap Child Safety Feedback

    Select the Child Safety Feedback option from the Profile menu.

  3. Submit your grievance

    Describe the concern with as much detail as possible, then submit. Our team reviews it from there.

You can also report by email

support@circledev.in

03 How reports are handled

CircleApp reviews every report of a potential CSAE violation and takes action based on the circumstances and applicable law.

This can include removing the prohibited content, restricting or terminating the account involved, preserving relevant information where legally appropriate, and reporting suspected child sexual exploitation to the appropriate authorities when required by law.

04 Account enforcement

Accounts found to be involved in CSAE or other child-safety violations may be suspended or permanently removed from CircleApp.

We also act against accounts that try to evade enforcement or repeatedly violate our child-safety requirements.

05 Cooperation with authorities

CircleApp may cooperate with law enforcement and relevant authorities regarding suspected child sexual abuse and exploitation, in accordance with applicable laws and legal requirements.

06 Prevention and user safety

We maintain safeguards designed to prevent and address child sexual abuse and exploitation on CircleApp.

If you come across suspicious behavior or content, report it rather than engaging with or redistributing it.

07 Contact

For questions about this policy, or to report a child-safety concern, reach out to us directly.
